# Methodology changelog

Full registration reasons, preserved verbatim from `dashboard/spec.py`. Rule values remain in that module; historical snapshots keep their original versions.

## 2026-10-13.1

Initial pre-registered rules, written before any dashboard results were computed.

## 2026-10-13.2

Data-integrity fix found in the first 25-stock smoke test, before any snapshot was published: a company with several listed share classes (e.g. GOOG and GOOGL) is evaluated once, using its most liquid class. No scoring rule, scale or weight changed.

## 2026-10-14.1

Owner requests (2026-10-14), written before any data was run with them: (1) only stocks Robinhood reports as tradable are published; a read-only Robinhood check no older than 35 days is required, and unverified stocks are excluded with that reason. (2) New list: long-horizon multibagger research candidates (5-10 years), $500M-$50B, with growth, margin, track-record, dilution and survival gates, a long-horizon weight profile with a new runway and operating-leverage component, and 10-year scenarios. The existing lists' rules, scales and weights did not change; fixed scales mean removing untradable stocks leaves the order of the others unchanged.

## 2026-10-14.2

Owner redesign request (2026-10-14), written before any data was evaluated with it: (1) three growth lists of up to 10: large cap (S&P 500 members), mid cap ($2B-$10B) and a new small-cap list ($300M-$2B; the former 'speculative' list's price and liquidity floors, without its 20% growth gate, and scored like the other growth lists); (2) the long-horizon list is renamed 'Speculative multibagger research candidates' and shows up to 5; (3) options cover calls AND puts, up to 10 per type per horizon (was 5 calls), with the same windows, liquidity filters and per-underlying limit; put deltas use their absolute value; (4) a separate owner watchlist with conditional year-end-2030 price scenarios (companies, unleveraged ETFs and leveraged ETFs each with their own method). Scoring scales and weights did not change.

## 2026-10-14.3

Watchlist only, from the owner's watchlist requirements (connect margins to per-share value): each company scenario now carries an explicit free-cash-flow margin path to 2030 (today's TTM FCF margin, or operating margin when FCF is unavailable, plus the scenario's margin shift) and the EV/FCF multiple that the 2030 value implies. A value with negative 2030 FCF or an implied EV/FCF above 60x is flagged as not supported by cash generation. Scenario prices, growth lists, options and scoring are unchanged.

## 2026-10-14.4

Data-gap fix approved by the owner on 2026-10-14 after the first live snapshot (2026-10-13), not to improve tracked results: companies tag diluted weighted-average shares for a fiscal fourth quarter only as a full-year figure, so the share-count change was missing whenever the latest quarter was a fiscal Q4 (seen for MU, STX and WDC). That scored the dilution input as 0 and failed the multibagger dilution gate as 'input unavailable'. Dilution now uses the newest quarter that has a quarterly diluted share count and the same quarter a year earlier (at most one quarter back). Scales, weights, gates and every other rule are unchanged; the 2026-10-13 snapshot stays as published under 2026-10-14.3.

## 2026-10-14.5

Owner request (2026-10-14), registered before any snapshot was evaluated with it. Display and data-quality rules only: no ranking rule, scale, weight, gate, threshold or list size changes, so the published lists and scores are unaffected. (1) Watchlist: the as-of price is validated on its own (WATCHLIST_PRICE_RULE); too little history no longer hides a verified price, and only the analyses that need history become unavailable, each naming the missing input. (2) Return windows are stored with their start and end sessions, and list sparklines use the same 1-year window and figures as the return table. (3) Freshness is counted in NYSE sessions in New York time (FRESHNESS_RULE). (4) Each dataset gets one state (DATASET_STATES). (5) A company research panel with deterministic trend, forensic (FORENSIC_RULES) and thesis checks: thesis conditions are those recorded when a name first appeared on a list (THESIS_TRACKING_RULE). (6) 'What changed since the previous snapshot?' (CHANGE_RULES). (7) A comparison page (COMPARE_RULES) and an interactive scenario calculator whose results are never stored or ranked (SCENARIO_LAB_BOUNDS). Several structures are adapted from filingcopilot, ticker-dossier and cc-equity-research (see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## 2026-10-14.6

Owner request (2026-10-14), registered before any snapshot was evaluated with it. Options watchlist: (1) each of the six option lists (calls and puts x 3, 6 and 12 months) shows up to 5 contracts instead of 10 (OPTION_RULES max_per_type_per_horizon); the windows, liquidity, spread, delta, standard-contract and per-underlying rules and the ranking weights are unchanged, and a list with fewer qualifying contracts is never padded. (2) Correctness: a contract whose theta is missing (or reported as exactly zero) is not eligible, with the reason 'theta unavailable'; before this version a missing theta became zero and earned the maximum time-decay score (OPTION_THETA_RULE). (3) Quote freshness is validated from the chain's own snapshot timestamp (UTC, converted to the New York session date) as well as the underlying's last trade; a contract's last trade time is reported separately and never used as its quote time (OPTION_QUOTE_RULE). (4) A contract's earnings status is 'unknown' when the calendar was not collected, never 'none scheduled' (OPTION_EVENT_RULE). (5) Probability of profit is defined (PROBABILITY_OF_PROFIT_DEFINITION) and gated: a percentage may be shown only for a calibration record that passes PROBABILITY_GATE; none exists, so every contract shows PROBABILITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED. (6) Payoffs state their fee treatment (FEE_TREATMENT). Display rules: the options page is always listed with its status; scenario-test flags are shown separately from passing the research screen; pages name both the snapshot's methodology version and the renderer version.

## 2026-10-14.7

Owner request (2026-10-14), registered before any snapshot was evaluated with it. New AI page with two new lists: (1) AI-exposed large cap (market cap of $10B or more) and AI-exposed mid cap ($2B to $10B), up to 5 stocks each, drawn from a fixed, curated universe of US-listed common stocks (AI_UNIVERSE) and evaluated by exactly the same data, liquidity, tradability and fixed-scale scoring rules as the growth lists (standard profile, same scales and weights); membership is a recorded judgement, and changing it needs a new version. (2) A top-20 market-cap scenario test (AI_TOP20_RULE): each listed AI stock's bear, base and bull scenario market caps at 5 and 10 years are compared with today's 20th-largest S&P 500 market cap, conditional and never a probability. (3) A context section for private AI companies (AI_PRIVATE_RULE): sourced, dated, labelled-unverified valuations from config/ai_private_companies.json; not ranked, scored or tradable. No probability of reaching the top 20 is shown, because none has out-of-sample calibration (PROBABILITY_GATE). The existing lists, scales, weights, gates, option rules and tracking rules are unchanged; the 2026-10-13 snapshot stays as published under 2026-10-14.3. Display and reliability changes in the same release (lazy evidence rows, sortable header buttons, saved filters) carry no methodology change.

## 2026-10-14.8

Owner request (2026-10-14), registered before any explorer data was evaluated. A separate small-bet options explorer uses the union of large, ai_large and ai_mid weekly candidates. Core uses existing OPTION_RULES. Long-shot requires 0.10 <= absolute delta < 0.30, open interest >= 200, volume >= 10, positive bid and ask above bid, spread <= 20% of mid, positive IV, nonzero theta and standard 100-share roots. Both use the existing 70-110, 150-210 and 300-430 day windows. Per tier, type, horizon and premium band, keep at most 12 contracts by the unchanged research score, ties by underlying, expiration, strike and OCC symbol. Ask x 100 premium bands are [0,100), [100,250), [250,500), [500,1000], and (1000,infinity). No padding, no per-underlying limit in the explorer, no short-dated contracts. Existing weekly top-five lists, OPTION_RULES, ranking weights, stock scores and tracking are unchanged. Browser budget filters and local slips do not rank or upload choices. No probability is established; no unsupported expiry-frequency assertion is made.

## 2026-10-14.9

Owner decision-research request, registered before any data is evaluated with these rules. Future large/mid/small growth lists use fundamental_growth: growth 25%, earnings, quality, balance and valuation 18.75% each, with existing scales/gates. Price momentum and relative performance are displayed separately, never added to this score. Other lists and existing option explorers stay unchanged. Extended means 1Y price return >150%. Annual revenue/EPS CAGR uses matched reported fiscal years 3/5 years apart; positive endpoints required. Quarterly growth trend compares latest YoY with previous-quarter YoY, stable within 2pp. Margin trends compare TTM with prior TTM, stable within 1pp. Valuation percentiles use same-list nonmissing observations, average ties, 0-100, at least two observations. Current Alpha Vantage estimates and six-calendar-month reported insider activity are display-only, never historical backfills or scoring inputs. SEC 10-K excerpts are dated and attributed; unsupported fields remain n/a. Up to 61 Alpha Vantage attempts per weekly run at existing pacing; daily earnings remain one request. New speculative options section uses top three candidates in each growth list, up to three contracts per ticker, 30-90 DTE, absolute delta 0.25-0.40, OI >500, volume >=10, positive two-sided markets, spread <=10% of mid, positive IV, nonzero theta and standard 100-share roots. Select by existing score, ties by expiry, strike, type, OCC. IV proxy averages ATM call/put IV in the nearest 20-40 DTE expiry to 30 days; IV rank needs 252 stored comparable sessions in 366 days. Earnings move uses ATM IV at the first expiry on/after the event within 90 days times sqrt(days/365). Put/call ratio is standard-contract 30-90 DTE volume. Local hypothetical budgets default to 5% overall and 1% per trade, with warnings, no storage/upload and no trading-limit changes. Exit examples are +100%, -50% and 21 DTE, never automated or claimed optimal.
