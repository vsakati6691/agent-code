## 2026-10-17.4

Owner-approved data fix: Cboe bars used as published; open not range-checked; lagging as-of bar from a verified close quote.

Owner-approved on 2026-10-17 after both weekly runs for the 2026-10-16 session failed with "benchmark IJH unavailable: price data rejected: IJH: open outside high/low range". Registered before any weekly snapshot exists under 2026-10-17.2 or 2026-10-17.3 and before any collection under these rules; it is a data-acceptance fix, not a response to results. Since 2026-10-17 the Cboe history parser no longer repairs bars, and the validator rejected any series with one open outside its high-low range anywhere in its history. In a 51-symbol sample of Cboe histories (benchmarks, large caps and the current option underlyings), every series was rejected: about 2% of all bars since 2014 have an open outside the published range (about 3% a year since 2023; median gap 0.08% of the close, 99th percentile about 1.5%), and 39 of the 51 series have one within the last 63 sessions. Closes outside the range were 21 bars, all from 2020 to 2022. Rules (PRICE_BAR_RULE): bars are used as published and nothing is repaired or invented; the open must stay positive and finite and still feeds the unadjusted-split check, but is not range-checked, because no score, list or displayed figure uses it; the close must lie within the high-low range in the last 252 sessions (PRICE_RANGE_CHECK_BARS, the 52-week-high window), and older breaks are kept and counted in the price flags. Cboe history files can lag the close by a session (on 2026-10-17 they still ended 2026-10-15). The as-of bar is then taken from the end-of-session quote only when that quote passed the existing post-close check, its last trade is in the as-of session, it reports a positive open, high, low and close and a volume with the close inside its own range, the history file ends on the preceding NYSE session, and the quote's close less its reported change matches that history close within $0.005 (QUOTE_PREV_CLOSE_TOLERANCE). The quote's own previous-close field is not used: after hours Cboe can roll it to the session's close (on 2026-10-17 it did for SPY, IWM, NVDA, FIX and QNST), while close less change still equalled the 2026-10-15 history close for all 13 quotes checked. A close-only or unlinked quote still cannot supply the bar, and MAX_PRICE_LAG_BUSINESS_DAYS stays 0. WATCHLIST_PRICE_RULE now refers to PRICE_BAR_RULE (previous text kept as WATCHLIST_PRICE_RULE_2026_10_14_5). The rule covers the public Cboe history only: private Massive history (2026-10-17.3, PRIVATE_HISTORY_RULE) keeps every existing range check and is never completed from a Cboe quote. The trading system's validator defaults are unchanged. No scale, weight, gate, list, scenario, option, tracking rule or request budget changes. Stored snapshots stay immutable; freeze date remains 2026-10-16.

## 2026-10-17.5

Owner request (2026-10-17): a Buy/Sell desk tab that reaches a rating with the same five stages as TradingAgents (TauricResearch/TradingAgents, Apache-2.0; arXiv 2412.20138), with each stage a fixed rule instead of a language model. The rules were written before any rating was computed and were not changed afterwards. Four analysts vote +1, 0 or -1, or abstain when inputs are missing: fundamentals (stored fundamental quality score), technical (50/200-day averages, MACD(12,26,9) and RSI(14) from the stored 253 daily closes), sentiment and positioning (SEC Form 4 open-market insider purchases, FINRA short interest as a share of diluted shares and the provider's analyst target; no social media) and news and events (revenue-growth, operating-margin and FCF-margin trends from the latest 10-Q/10-K; no headlines). Fewer than three voting analysts means not rated. The net vote decides the bull/bear debate and the trader's five-level proposal (Buy, Overweight, Hold, Underweight, Sell). Four risk flags (earnings within 10 days, the stored extended flag, annualised volatility above 60%, balance-sheet component below 0.30) let aggressive, neutral and conservative reviewers step Buy or Overweight down, never below Hold; the portfolio manager takes the median review and caps it at Hold on low evidence confidence. Full thresholds: DESK_RULE and DESK_RULES. Inputs are fields every snapshot already stores: no new provider, host, request budget or dataset. Ratings are stored in each new weekly snapshot as decision_desk and tracked by rating as excess price return over the entry's benchmark (DESK_TRACKING); site builds from older snapshots show labelled previews that are never tracked. Ratings are rule-based research labels, not advice, with no established probability or edge. No existing scale, weight, gate, list, scenario, option rule or list-tracking rule changes.
